Three Directions Worth Building

Research agenda
1

Build more verifiers

More RL environments, deeper coverage — and go on-site to learn what "good" actually means in each domain, not just academic benchmarks.

2

Audit verifier trust

Check the verifier in the conditions the model will actually face. A leaky verifier teaches the model to exploit the leak, not to do the task.

3

Spend real tests wisely

Real-world tests are capital-intensive and slow. Simulate what can be simulated; when measuring, pick the experiments whose results most improve the next round.

Deployment matters: unlike the digital world, physical-world data is scarce and scattered — systems must work in the field to keep collecting data, improving verifiers, and learning what the problem really is.
